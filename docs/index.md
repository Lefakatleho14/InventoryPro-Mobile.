# Privacy Policy for InventoryPro

InventoryPro was developed as a student project at IIE Rosebank. 

We collect your email address solely for the purpose of Firebase Authentication (logging in). We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Inventory data entered into the app is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore and is only accessible to authenticated users of your organization.
